# Design System Specification: The Ethereal Sanctuary

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
This design system is built upon a philosophy of **"Tactile Serenity."** Moving away from the rigid, boxed-in layouts of standard web frameworks, this system treats the screen as a high-end editorial canvas. It balances the warmth of human connection with the precision of premium healthcare and wellness aesthetics.

**Creative North Star: The Organic Gallery**
The goal is to create a digital environment that feels like a curated physical space—airy, intentional, and soft. We achieve this by breaking the "template" look through intentional asymmetry, overlapping elements that create depth, and a typographic scale that prioritizes breathing room over information density. Every element should feel like it was placed by hand, not generated by a grid.

---

## 2. Colors: Tonal Depth & Warmth
The palette is a sophisticated blend of grounded slates, soft blushes, and clinical whites. We use color to direct attention and define space, rather than using structural lines.

### The Palette
- **Primary (`#99415a` / `#f78da7`):** Use the deeper wine tone for text-based actions and the soft pink blush for large surfaces and primary buttons.
- **Secondary (`#4F5A60` / `#556066`):** A slate blue-grey that provides professional authority.
- **Neutral/Surface (`#f4faff` to `#ffffff`):** A range of cool-tinted whites and surgical blues that keep the interface feeling sterile yet welcoming.

### Rules for Application
*   **The "No-Line" Rule:** 1px solid borders are strictly prohibited for sectioning. Boundaries must be defined solely through background color shifts. For example, a `surface_container_low` section should sit directly against a `surface` background to define its start and end.
*   **Surface Hierarchy & Nesting:** Treat the UI as layers of fine paper. Use `surface_container_lowest` (#ffffff) for floating cards to create a "lift" against the `surface` background (#f4faff).
*   **The "Glass & Gradient" Rule:** To avoid a flat, "standard" feel, use Glassmorphism for floating navigation bars (70% opacity with a 20px backdrop blur). Main CTAs should utilize a subtle linear gradient from `primary` (#99415a) to `primary_container` (#f78da7) at a 135-degree angle.

---

## 3. Typography: Editorial Authority
The typography leverages **Montserrat** for its geometric clarity and **Yu Gothic** for its sophisticated, high-legibility Japanese and Sans-serif texture.

*   **Display & Headlines:** Use `display-lg` (3.5rem) and `headline-lg` (2rem) with generous letter spacing (-0.02em). These should be treated as "hero" elements, often placed asymmetrically to drive the editorial feel.
*   **Body Copy:** Use `body-lg` (1rem) for general reading. Ensure a line height of at least 1.7 to maintain the "airy" brand profile. 
*   **Labels:** `label-md` (0.75rem) should be used sparingly for metadata, always in uppercase with +0.05em tracking to ensure a premium, "stamped" look.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth
In this design system, depth is a result of **Tonal Layering** rather than artificial light sources.

*   **The Layering Principle:** Stacking is the primary tool for hierarchy. A `surface_container_highest` element implies high interactive priority.
*   **Ambient Shadows:** When a "floating" effect is required (e.g., a modal), use a shadow with a 40px blur and 4% opacity. The shadow color must be a tinted version of the `on_surface` color (#121d22), never pure black.
*   **The "Ghost Border" Fallback:** If a container requires definition against an identical color background, use a "Ghost Border"—the `outline_variant` token at 15% opacity. 
*   **Glassmorphism:** Use semi-transparent surface colors for overlays. This allows the organic shapes of the background to bleed through, softening the edges of the UI and making the layout feel integrated.

## 5. Components

### Buttons
*   **Primary:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). Gradient fill from `primary` to `primary_container`. White text. No border.
*   **Secondary:** Pill-shaped. `secondary_container` background with `on_secondary_container` text.
*   **Tertiary:** Text-only with a 2px underline that appears on hover, utilizing the `primary` color.

### Cards & Lists
*   **Cards:** No borders or dividers. Use `surface_container_low` for the card body and `surface_container_lowest` for internal elements (like image wrappers). Use `spacing-xl` (1.5rem) as internal padding to ensure content never feels cramped.
*   **Lists:** Forbid the use of horizontal divider lines. Separate items using vertical white space and subtle background shifts on hover.

### Form Inputs
*   **Text Fields:** Use a subtle `surface_variant` background rather than a white box. The bottom-only border (2px) should use `outline_variant` and transition to `primary` on focus.
*   **Selection (Radio/Check):** Use the `primary_container` (#f78da7) for the "selected" state. Soften the corners of checkboxes to `0.25rem` to match the friendly brand profile.

### Interactive Tooltips
*   **Styling:** Use `inverse_surface` with `inverse_on_surface` text. Apply a `md` (0.75rem) corner radius.

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do
*   **Do** use asymmetrical layouts where imagery overlaps text containers.
*   **Do** prioritize white space as a functional element, not just "empty" space.
*   **Do** use high-quality, warm-toned photography to balance the cool slate colors.
*   **Do** ensure all interactive elements have a minimum touch target of 44px, despite the delicate visual style.

### Don't
*   **Don't** use 100% black (#000000). Use `on_surface` (#121d22) for high-contrast text.
*   **Don't** use sharp corners. Everything must adhere to the `roundedness scale` (minimum 0.5rem).
*   **Don't** use standard "drop shadows" or heavy inner shadows. Stick to tonal shifts.
*   **Don't** overcrowd the viewport. If a section feels busy, double the padding.
